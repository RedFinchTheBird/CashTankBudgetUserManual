CashTank Financial Planner

The financial planner in CashTank Budget projects your finances year by year, from today to the end of your life expectancy. It shows whether your money lasts and how much tax you pay along the way. It can also try hundreds of retirement, Social Security and Roth conversion choices to find the plan that holds up best.

What the planner does

  • Simulates every year of your plan: investment growth and yield, wages, pensions, Social Security, required minimum distributions, Roth conversions, living expenses, income taxes and Medicare premiums.
  • Fills in your tax forms for every year (Form 1040 and the Social Security benefits worksheet), so you can see where each dollar of tax comes from.
  • Tests a bad market. Besides a "normal" market built from long-run averages, it replays real 2000-2026 market history starting at the worst point for your mix of investments.
  • Compares your choices. Each Run Plan runs every combination of your retirement ages, Social Security claim ages, Roth conversion buckets and both market scenarios, and stars the one that lasts longest. You choose which scenario to view.

Screens built and in progress

The planner is still being built. Each screen chapter is marked:

  • Built - the screen works as described and the pictures are real screenshots.
  • Preview - the calculations behind the screen are finished, but the screen itself is still being designed. The pictures are mockups and details may change.
An educational tool, not advice The planner uses 2026 tax rules and simplifies some of them (see assumptions and simplifications). Real markets will not repeat history exactly. Use the results to compare choices and ask better questions, and check important decisions with a tax or financial professional.
