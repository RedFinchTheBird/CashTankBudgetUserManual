Plan milestones (year codes)

An income or expense often starts or stops at an event - when you retire, when you claim Social Security, at the end of life. Instead of typing a year, choose a milestone. Milestones move automatically when you change an age, and in every scenario Run Plan tries.

The milestones

CodeMeaningYear
SOPStart of planThe current year (plan year 1)
YORTP1Year of retirement, tax payer 1Year of birth + retirement age
YORTP2Year of retirement, tax payer 2Year of birth + retirement age
YORALLBoth retiredThe later of the two retirements
SSCTP1Social Security claim, tax payer 1Year of birth + claim age
SSCTP2Social Security claim, tax payer 2Year of birth + claim age
SSCALLBoth claimingThe later of the two claims
YODTP1Life expectancy year, tax payer 1Year of birth + life expectancy
YODTP2Life expectancy year, tax payer 2Year of birth + life expectancy
YODALLLast life expectancy year (end of plan)The later of the two

"ALL" always means the later of the two tax payers. With one tax payer, the TP2 and ALL codes mean the same as the TP1 codes, and the picker only offers SOP, YORTP1, SSCTP1 and YODTP1.

The start and end year picker

  • Plan milestones (top) - each code with the year it falls in today, each tax payer's age that year and what it means.
  • Calendar years (bottom) - every year of the plan with both ages and what happens that year: retirements, Social Security claims, the start of RMDs and life expectancy years (in bold).
  • Select one entry and click OK (or double-click it). Choosing in one list clears the other.

The grid shows a milestone with its current year, for example YORTP1 (2026); a calendar year shows just the year.

Milestones move, calendar years don't

The years shown use each tax payer's first retirement age and claim age. When a scenario uses retirement age 65 instead of 62, every YORTP1 in your income and expenses moves three years later for that scenario. A calendar year such as 2030 stays put.

Which to use Use a milestone when the date really depends on the event: a salary that ends at retirement, a bridge expense until Social Security starts, travel in the first years of retirement. Use a calendar year for fixed dates such as a mortgage payoff or a child's college years.

Details

  • Start and end years are both included: an expense from YORTP1 to YORTP1 happens in the retirement year only.
  • New income and expenses start at SOP and end at YODALL.
  • Wages always stop at retirement and pensions always start at it, whatever the end and start years say.
  • If tax payer 2 is deleted, dates using their milestones switch to the matching tax payer 1 milestone.
