Plan results Preview

Run Plan calculates every scenario. On Plan Results you choose one scenario and read every year of it from several angles.

Choose a scenario

The first time you click Plan Results after Run Plan, the Choose a Plan Scenario dialog lists every scenario Run Plan tried. No scenario is selected for you - you decide which one to look at.

  • Pick one. Click a row, then View Results, or double-click the row.
  • The best scenario is starred, shown in green, and summarized above the list. Its Select it link selects it in the list. See how the best scenario is chosen.
  • Red rows are scenarios where the money runs out before the end of the plan; Plan ends is the year it ran out.
  • Market shows all scenarios, or only the normal or only the down and flat market ones. It starts at All.
  • Sorting. The list starts sorted by tax payer 1's retirement age, then Plan ends, then NAV at end, all highest first. Click a column heading to sort by it.
  • Cancel leaves Plan Results without a scenario; its Choose Scenario... button opens the list again.
ColumnMeaning
ScenarioThe scenario number (1, 2, 3...).
Name retires / Name claimsEach tax payer's retirement age and Social Security claim age in this scenario. With one tax payer the second pair is hidden.

Change the scenario

  • The banner at the top of Plan Results names the scenario every view shows: its number, market, how long the money lasts, and each tax payer's ages and Roth bucket.
  • Change Scenario... opens the scenario list again with your current scenario selected. Choose another and every view switches to it.
  • Once you have chosen, clicking Plan Results goes straight to that scenario.
  • After the next Run Plan, no scenario is selected, so Plan Results opens the list again.
  • If you change the plan after Run Plan, the banner says the results are out of date. Click Run Plan to recalculate them.

Annual Cash Flows

Every movement of money in every year: wages and pensions into accounts, Social Security and RMDs into the cash account, Roth conversions, and each withdrawal that paid an expense, a tax or Medicare.

ColumnMeaning
Year, agesThe calendar year and each tax payer's age.
DescriptionThe income or expense name, or Social Security, Required Minimum Distribution, Roth Conversion, Federal Income Tax, State Income Tax, Medicare Premiums.
From, ToWhere the money came from and went: a tax payer, a pension, an account, Auto, Living Expenses, a tax or Medicare. Renaming an account or tax payer renames it here too.
AmountThe amount in that year's dollars.
1040 boxWhere the flow appears on Form 1040 (1a wages, 4a IRA, 5a pensions and 401(k), 6a Social Security).

Annual Account Balances

One row per account per year: starting balance, money in (deposits), money out (withdrawals) and ending balance. Ending minus starting, minus in, plus out is that year's market growth and yield.

Annual Income, Expense Table

Each income and expense item per year, including Social Security, federal and state income tax and Medicare premiums. The income tax in a year is the tax paid that year - last year's return; the Annual Tax Forms view shows the year's own return. RMDs and Roth conversions are not listed here, because they move your own money between accounts rather than add or spend it.

Annual Income, Expense Graph

Total income (wages, pensions and Social Security) against total expenses (living expenses, the taxes paid that year and Medicare) for each year. Years where expenses are above income are paid from savings.

Annual Net Asset Value Graph

Net asset value (NAV) is the total of all your accounts at the end of each year. A NAV that falls toward zero before the end of the plan is a warning sign; if it reaches the point where a bill cannot be paid, the plan ends that year.

For every year, the Form 1040 lines and the Social Security benefits worksheet, as the planner filled them in. The federal tax on line 16 is paid the next year.
